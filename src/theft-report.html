After a theft, make this report and send it to the CA Lottery so they can stop the stolen tickets. It lists every pack's game, pack number and the tickets still unsold at the last count (shift close or close).

Live Packs in Slots

Unsold tickets of each pack at its newest count: today's last shift close, else the last close, or when it was put in if that's later. Each pack says which.

Back Stock

Sealed packs in the back. Pack numbers for back stock aren't tracked yet, so only game, packs and tickets.
